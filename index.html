How to join

Group Text is a private text-message group for friends and volunteers who coordinate events and share updates. One shared number relays each member's message to the rest of the group, labeled with the sender's name.

To opt in, text the word JOIN to (913) 513-1116.

By texting JOIN you agree to receive text messages from Group Text at the mobile number you texted from. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

After you text JOIN you will receive a confirmation reply restating these terms. You are only added to the group after you opt in yourself. Nobody can be added by another person without texting JOIN.

Example messages:

  • "Group Text: Welcome! You're subscribed to group messages. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel."
  • "Group Text – Maria: Reminder, setup starts at 9am Saturday. Reply to message the group."
  • "Group Text – Tom: I can bring extra chairs."

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who we are. Group Text is operated by Gerardo Lopez, an individual, ("we", "us").

Information we collect. When you text JOIN, we collect your mobile phone number, your name (if you provide it), and the content of messages you send to the group.

How we use it. We use this information only to operate the group text service: delivering your messages to the other members, showing your name as the sender, and responding to your requests such as STOP and HELP.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or other mobile opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number and messages are visible to us and to our messaging and technology providers (such as Twilio) solely so they can deliver the service. Members of the group will see your name and the messages you send.

Retention and your choices. You can leave at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to delete your information by contacting us below.

Security. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children. The service is not directed to children under 13.

Changes. We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
